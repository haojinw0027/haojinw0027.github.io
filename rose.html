Method Overview

ROSE: a warm-up strategy for On-Policy Distillation

On-Policy Distillation (OPD) aligns the student to the teacher through progressive alignment on high-probability tokens at student-visited states. However, OPD's effectiveness depends on a critical precondition: sufficient top-k token overlap between student and teacher distributions. When the student model is too weak on hard problems, its top-k tokens diverge significantly from the teacher's — the probability mass concentrates on a narrow, non-overlapping set (Li et al., 2026). Without overlap, OPD produces no effective gradient signal, and training stalls.

ROSE addresses this cold-start problem as an off-policy warm-up for OPD. By stitching teacher continuations onto student-generated prefixes, ROSE gradually shifts the student's token distribution closer to the teacher's — increasing top-k overlap so that subsequent OPD can operate effectively.
