Potential Research Direction

What I'd Like to Explore

1. Continual Expansion of Reasoning Capacity in the Most Online Way

Online RL is preferable for continual learning — it avoids catastrophic forgetting by keeping the model close to its current distribution. However, hard problems receive no reward signal under RLVR, forcing a fall-back to offline learning. In that offline regime, on-policy SFT methods like RFT struggle to sample any correct trajectory at all.

I'm interested in exploring minimally offline guidance as a remedy: either using a MENTORCOLLAB-style approach — injecting the smallest necessary amount of off-policy data to rescue RFT on hard problems — or augmenting the online phase with dense intermediate rewards to sidestep the sparse-reward failure mode entirely. The goal is to keep training as online as possible while still making progress on problems at the frontier of the model's current capability, enabling genuine continual expansion of reasoning capacity.

2. Warming Up for On-Policy Distillation

I believe optimization for better on-policy distillation (OPD) should aim at closing the distribution gap between teacher and student. When the student's rollout distribution diverges too far from the teacher's, supervision signal becomes noisy and training inefficient. A key open question is therefore how to imitate the teacher effectively from limited data — designing offline warm-up objectives that bring the student close enough to the teacher's manifold before online learning begins.

3. Agentic / Multi-Turn RL

This is a direction I am still actively exploring. My intuition is that the core idea behind ROSE — using an on-policy failing trajectory prefix paired with off-policy teacher guidance as the supervision target — should transfer naturally to agentic and multi-turn settings, where the student agent frequently stalls or goes off-track mid-episode. Stitching teacher continuations at failure points could provide denser, more grounded training signal than sparse end-of-episode rewards alone.
